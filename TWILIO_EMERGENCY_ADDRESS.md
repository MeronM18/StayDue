# Twilio Emergency Address Setup

## What This Means

Twilio requires an emergency address for phone numbers to comply with regulations. If someone calls 911/emergency services from your Twilio number, Twilio needs to know where to route the call.

**The $75 fee**: This is charged ONLY if an emergency call is made from your number. For development/testing, this is extremely unlikely.

## What to Do

### Step 1: Accept the Terms

1. **Check the box**: "I agree to comply with the Emergency calling terms and conditions"
2. **Click Continue** or **Purchase** (depending on the button)

### Step 2: Add Emergency Address (After Getting Number)

After you get your phone number, you'll need to add an emergency address:

1. Go to **Phone Numbers** → **Manage** → **Active Numbers**
2. Click on your phone number
3. Scroll to **"Emergency Address"** section
4. Click **"Add Address"** or **"Edit"**
5. Fill in:
   - **Street Address**: Your street address (or business address)
   - **City**: Your city
   - **State/Province**: Your state or province
   - **Postal Code**: Your postal code
   - **Country**: Your country
6. Click **"Save"** or **"Verify"**

### Step 3: Verify Address (If Required)

Twilio may require address verification:
- They might send a verification code via mail (rare)
- Or verify via automated service
- Follow the prompts in Twilio dashboard

## Important Notes

- ✅ **You can use your real address** - This is kept private and only used for emergency routing
- ✅ **For development**: The emergency address is just a requirement - you won't use it unless someone calls 911
- ✅ **The $75 fee**: Only applies if an actual emergency call is made (extremely rare for development)
- ✅ **You can update the address later** if needed

## Quick Answer
